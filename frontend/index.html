How to Play AirChord

Position both hands in view of your camera. One hand selects chords; the other strums through the air.

C Major 1 Finger

Raise only your index finger. AirChord immediately locks in the bright, resonant sound of C Major.

G Major 2 Fingers

Raise your index and middle fingers (peace sign). Perfect for standard acoustic progressions.

D Major 3 Fingers

Raise index, middle, and ring fingers. Rich high-frequency guitar resonance.

E Minor 4 Fingers

Raise four fingers (tucking in thumb). Moody, deep acoustic chord with open low E string.

A Minor 5 Fingers

Open your entire palm with all five fingers spread. Warm melancholic guitar harmony.

Air Strumming Motion

Move your strumming hand vertically through the air. A brisk downward swipe triggers a downstroke; an upward flick triggers an upstroke.
